# Silkroad Atlas forum sources — 1 October 2026

The app now includes **26 forum/community websites**, represented by **28 advertisement sections** and **9 community references**, plus the two existing server directories.

The bundled catalog contains **1,661 listings**, **448 distinct public Discord invites**, and **1,206 distinct forum advertisement URLs** attached to **810 catalog records**. A listing is an observed advertisement, not proof of a currently running game server. Different announcements and server editions may remain separate.

## Use in the app

Open **Sources & settings**. Search by forum name or language; choose **Show imported listings** to browse its advertisements, **Refresh** to update that source, or **Open** to visit the original forum. Existing saved catalogs receive new built-in sources automatically. Disabled and removed sources stay under your control.

**Forum-advertisements.csv** contains imported forum-linked records with website, metadata, Discord and original announcement URLs. **Forum-sources.json** is a portable source inventory; the built-in definitions are in **Source/Core/ForumSources.json**. Rebuild the app after editing built-in definitions, or use Add source in the app.

## Import coverage

Collection followed up to 40 index pages per advertising section and read up to 12 announcement first posts per section for additional links. Most accessible sections ended before the page limit. Source statuses below identify blocks, partial results and unavailable posts. Forum rules, common help topics, quoted messages and reply signatures are excluded. Reference communities are available as links only, because a dedicated advertising section or reliable importer was not confirmed.

The app stores titles, extracted facts and links; it does not republish complete forum posts. Public Discord invites may expire; use Check invites & load icons for available public community artwork and current invite details. Private channels and login-only content are not indexed.

This is a researched source collection, not an exhaustive registry of every Silkroad forum. Archived, unindexed, inaccessible and newly created communities may be missing.
